Diagnosis — why dinosaurs float & teleport

Root causes confirmed in-engine
Critical

NPCs teleport, not walk

The brain moves each NPC with PivotTo(CFrame.lookAt()) in discrete 8-stud jumps once per second. No interpolation between ticks → visible snapping/teleport.

Critical

No physics, no rig

Sample NPC = 104 parts, all anchored, no Humanoid, no Animator. Anchored bodies ignore gravity, so any altitude reads as "static floating."

High

Zero animation

NPC models contain 0 Animation objects and no AnimationController. Legs never move — a sliding statue even when position changes.

High

Brain runs on a 1s server tick

TickSeconds = 1 drives all sensing & movement. Even with smoothing, decisions feel laggy. Movement cadence and decision cadence are coupled.

Opportunity

Old NPCs are primitive CSG

Gameplay spawns the block/union-built dinos — not the new packs. The rigged mesh dinos sit unused in Workspace, ungoverned by NPCService.

Opportunity

Combat is data-only

Attacks resolve as server attribute math (CombatService) with no hit feedback, animation, or telegraph — functional, but invisible to the player.
